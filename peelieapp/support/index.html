help & support

How can we help?

Answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find yours? Email teampeelie@wcloud.nz, or in the app go to Settings → Contact Peelie. A real person will get back to you.

Getting started

How do I sign in?

With Sign in with Apple, or with your email. If you use email, we send you a sign-in link and a 6-digit code. Tap the link, or type the code into the app if you opened the email on another device. The email comes from “Peelie”. There are no passwords to remember.

Do I need to pay?

No. Peelie is free for customers, always. Only shops can choose to pay, for Peelie Pro.

Which devices does Peelie work on?

iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo (including the Duo's outer screen). Peelie isn't on Android yet.

What kinds of businesses use Peelie?

Any business people come back to: cafés, restaurants and bakeries, but also hair and nail salons, barbers, massage and day spas, photography studios, bookshops, gift and toy shops. Each business sets its own reward, how many stickers fill a card, and a minimum spend.

Where is Peelie available?

Peelie will be on the App Store worldwide, and we're launching with shops in Ōtautahi Christchurch, New Zealand.

Collecting stickers

How do I get a sticker?

At the counter, open Peelie, tap Get my sticker and show your code to the staff. They scan it with the shop's phone or iPad (or type the code under the QR, like PL-7K2QX9), and the sticker lands on your card. Location isn't needed.

When do I take the photo?

Whenever your order arrives, or once you've got your new find. Take a photo and Peelie turns it into a sticker, or pick one of ours instead. If you don't get to it, after 24 hours the spot becomes the shop's own sticker, so you never lose a stamp.

How do I claim my reward?

When a reward is ready, open the shop's card and tap Claim my reward. Show your code to the staff and they'll scan it, just like a sticker.

Why can't I get another sticker yet?

It's one sticker per shop every 3 hours. That keeps things fair for the shop, and means a sticker is for a visit, not for each item.

Does Peelie need my location?

No, it's optional, and stickers work with it off. If you turn it on, Peelie uses it only on your phone while the app is open: to show “You're at [shop]” so the right card pops up at the counter, and to show shops near you on the Explore map. Your location is never sent to us or stored.

Peelie only asks when it's useful (opening Explore, or tapping Turn on in My cards). Switch it off any time in Settings → Card pops up at the shop, or in your iPhone's Settings.

Do I lose my stickers if I change phones?

No. Everything is saved to your account. Sign in on your new phone the same way as before and it's all there. You can also download your diary (photos and stickers) from Settings.

Privacy & safety

Can the shop see who I am?

Not unless you want them to. When they scan you, they see your customer code and your visits, stickers and rewards with them. They only see your name, email, phone and birthday if you switch on sharing for that shop in Settings → Who can see my name. It's off by default, and you can switch it off any time. Shops never see your photos.

How do I stop promotion notifications?

Promotions from a shop are off unless you turn them on. Change them shop by shop in Settings → Promotions, or turn off Peelie notifications in your iPhone's Settings.

How do I report or hide a post?

Tap ••• on the post, then Report this post and pick a reason (offensive, misleading or a scam, spam, or something else). The Peelie team reviews every report within 24 hours. To stop seeing a shop's posts, choose Hide posts from that shop. You can show them again from Settings or the shop's page.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete my account. Your cards, stickers, photos and diary are removed for good. See our privacy policy for the details.

For shops

How do I get my shop on Peelie?

Set up your shop in the Peelie app. The Peelie team checks new shops before they can post. If you'd like a hand, email teampeelie@wcloud.nz.

What does the free plan include?

Everything: the card designer, ads, notifications and analytics, for 1 staff member and up to 50 customers (50 different people with your card). When you reach 50, everyone who already has your card keeps collecting as normal. New customers can join once you upgrade, and your staff see a friendly “This shop's free plan is full” message.

How do I get Peelie Pro, and what does it cost?

In the app, go to Home → Get Peelie Pro. Pro gives you unlimited customers and unlimited staff. It's NZ$139.99 a year, and new subscribers pay NZ$69.99 for their first year (about NZ$5.83 a month). Apple sets the matching price in other countries.

It's paid through your Apple ID and renews each year unless you turn it off at least 24 hours before the year ends. Manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

We joined Peelie before plans started. Do we pay?

No. Shops that were on Peelie before plans started get Peelie Pro free for a year.

Do I need any equipment?

Just the Peelie app on a phone or iPad with a camera at the counter, which most shops already have. No scanners or till changes. You can print a window poster with your shop's QR code straight from the app.

How do staff give stickers?

At the start of a shift, each staff member types their own PIN on the shop's phone or iPad. After that they scan customers' codes and tap Give sticker. Every sticker is logged against whoever was on shift, and they can check out when they finish.

A staff member forgot their PIN, or has left

In the app, go to Staff and reset or remove their PIN. For security, a PIN is only shown once, when it's created or reset. After 5 wrong tries the PIN pad locks for 15 minutes on that device.

How does Peelie stop cheating?

Only signed-in staff on your own device can give stickers. There's one sticker per customer every 3 hours, a log of every shift, a shuffled PIN pad so nobody can learn a PIN by watching, and an alert if one staff member gives out an unusual number of stickers.

How do ads and notifications work?

Ads show on your card in the app. You can also send one as a notification to customers who've switched on your promotions, up to twice a week, so it never feels spammy.

How do I close my shop and delete my account?

In the app, go to your shop's Settings → Delete my account. This closes your shop on Peelie (its card, customers' stickers there, ads and staff) and deletes your account. If you have Peelie Pro, also cancel it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions so it doesn't renew.

Still stuck?

Email teampeelie@wcloud.nz, or in the app go to Settings → Contact Peelie. It helps if you tell us your device and what you were trying to do.
