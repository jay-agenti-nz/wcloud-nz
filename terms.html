4. Payment

We accept bank transfer. Work begins once payment or the deposit has cleared. Your website goes live once the balance is paid.

Each package includes the number of revision rounds published for it. A round means one consolidated set of changes from you, not a change at a time. Timelines assume you can give us what we need to do the work:

Delivery estimates are from when we have what we need, not from when you pay. If material or feedback is slow, the timeline moves — we will tell you when that happens rather than quietly missing a date.
