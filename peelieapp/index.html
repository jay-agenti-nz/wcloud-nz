How it works

Three taps at the counter. That's it.

No scanners, no cards to carry, no apps for staff to learn.

Tap “Get my sticker”

When you pay, open Peelie and hand your phone to the staff.

Staff type their PIN

A quick 4-digit code and the sticker's on your card. Done in seconds.

Snap your dish

When your food arrives, take a photo. Peelie cuts it out into a sticker.

Fill a card, get a treat.

Your food diary

Every sticker, kept for good.

Your stickers don't vanish when a card fills up. They live in a calendar of every meal you've had out, so you can look back at that brunch, or remember what you ordered last time.

  • A diary of your meals out, sorted by day
  • Find Peelie cafés near you
  • Deals from places you love, only if you switch them on
  • Your name stays private unless you choose to share it

For café & restaurant owners

Replace your paper card. Set up in minutes.

No hardware, no till integration, nothing to print except a poster for your window. Your regulars already have the phone. Peelie does the rest.

Your card, your way

Add your logo and colour, choose the reward, how many stickers fill a card, and a minimum spend.

A PIN for each staff member

Staff type a 4-digit PIN on the customer's phone. See who gave which sticker, and reset a PIN any time.

Know your regulars

A simple dashboard of visits and rewards, plus a list of customers who've chosen to share their name and email with you.

Deals, menu & pushes

Post deals and your menu. Send a notification to customers who've opted in, up to twice a week, so it never feels spammy.

A poster for your window

Print a ready-made poster with your own QR code. Customers scan it and your card's on their phone.

Cheat-proof by design

PINs are scrambled and locked after wrong guesses, one sticker per visit every 3 hours, a location check, and an alert if something looks off.

Privacy, done properly

Your details stay yours.

A café only sees your name and email if you switch on “Share my name & email” for that café. Deal alerts are off until you turn them on. Your food photos are private to you.

Peelie follows the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, and you can delete your account from inside the app at any time. Read the privacy policy.
