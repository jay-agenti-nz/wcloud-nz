how it works

Show your code. Snap your food. That's it.

No paper cards to lose, and nothing for the shop to install. Staff scan your code with the shop's own phone or iPad.

one

Show your code

At the counter, tap the Sticker button. Your QR code comes up.

two

Staff scan it

They scan your code, tap Give sticker, and it's on your card.

three

Snap what you got

Take a photo of your food and it becomes your sticker. Or pick one of ours.

fill the card

Every sticker gets you closer to a treat.

Each shop sets its own reward: a free coffee, a pastry, a curry of your choice. When your card's full, show your code again and the treat is yours.

  • Some shops add little rewards along the way
  • Peelie tells you when a reward is ready
  • Your full cards stay in your wallet, so nothing's lost

for café & shop owners

Replace your paper card. Set up in minutes.

No scanners, no till changes, nothing new to buy. Peelie runs on the phone or iPad you already have behind the counter, and your regulars already carry their card in their pocket.

Design your card

Pick a look, your colour or a photo, the reward, how many stickers fill a card, and a minimum spend.

Scan to give a sticker

Staff check in once a shift with their own PIN, then scan the customer's code. You can see who gave what.

Analytics in plain English

Visits, your busiest day, and where customers drop off, written as a short story about your month.

Ads & offers

Post an offer that shows on your card, free and unlimited. Send it as a notification to people who've opted in, up to twice a week.

A poster for your window

Print a poster with your own QR code. Customers scan it and your card's on their phone.

Cheat-proof

Only your staff can give stickers, one per customer every 3 hours, with a log of every shift and an alert if anything looks unusual.

made to feel like yours

A card that looks like your shop.

Choose from designs like Simple, Bistro, Garden, Waves, Gallery and Sunrise, then add your colours, logo or a photo. Swipe the card to see the back, with your address and links.

When you've got something on, write an ad in a minute and it shows on your card straight away.

privacy, done properly

Shops see your code, not you.

When staff scan you, they see a customer code and your stickers. They only see your name and email if you switch on sharing for that shop. Promotions are off until you turn them on, and your food photos are just for you.

Your location stays on your phone. Peelie follows the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, and you can delete your account in the app at any time. Read the privacy policy.
