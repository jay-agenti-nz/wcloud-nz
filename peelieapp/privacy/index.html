Peelie privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This explains what the Peelie app collects, why, where it's kept, who can see it, and how to delete it. We've written it to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version

  • We collect what Peelie needs to run your stamp cards, and nothing for advertising.
  • When staff scan you, the shop sees a customer code and your stickers, not your name. They only see your name and contact details if you switch on sharing for that shop.
  • Your food photos are private to you.
  • Your location stays on your phone. We don't collect or store it.
  • Everything is stored with Supabase in Sydney, Australia.
  • You can delete your account, and everything linked to it, from inside the app.

If you own a shop, we also collect your business details (name, address, phone, website, social links, logo, menu, ads and their photos, which are shown publicly in the app), your staff members' names and PINs, and a log of when each staff member started and ended a shift on your shop's phone or iPad, along with an identifier for that device. PINs are scrambled (hashed) so nobody, including us, can read them back.

We don't collect payment card details, we don't use advertising or tracking tools in the app, and we don't sell your information.

What shops can see

When staff scan your code, the shop sees your customer code, your stickers and rewards with them, and which of their staff gave each one. Shops also see overall counts, like how many people have their card. They don't see who you are.

If you switch on sharing for a shop (in Settings, Who can see my name), that shop can also see your name, email and phone number (if you've added one), your birthday (only if Birthday treats is on), and your visits to them. They can download that list. Once a shop has downloaded it, it's their responsibility to look after it under the Privacy Act.

You can switch sharing off at any time. Promotions from a shop are off unless you turn them on, and a shop can send at most two a week.

Shops never see your food photos, your location, or your stickers from other shops.

Where it's stored

Peelie's data and photos are stored with Supabase, in its Sydney, Australia data centre. That means your information leaves New Zealand. Australia has privacy laws that give comparable protection to ours, and Supabase is bound by its own security and privacy commitments (supabase.com/privacy).

Notifications are delivered through Apple's push notification service, and if you sign in with Apple, Apple handles that sign-in under Apple's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have a Peelie account, so your diary stays complete for as long as you want it. You can download a copy of your diary at any time from Settings.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings and tap Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, sticker photos, cards, stickers, rewards and privacy choices. Copies may stay in our encrypted backups for a short time until those backups are replaced.

If you own a shop, you'll need to hand over or close your shop in the app before you can delete your account. If you have trouble, email us and we'll do it for you.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, give you a copy, and correct anything that's wrong. Email kiaora@wcloud.nz and we'll reply within 20 working days. There's no charge.

If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects information you've already given us, we'll tell you in the app first.
