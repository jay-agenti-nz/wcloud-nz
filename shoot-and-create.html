Why I shoot

I’d be out there with a camera anyway.

Jay D Vera

Most weekends you’ll find me somewhere on the Port Hills — Godley Head, Taylors Mistake, Scarborough Bluffs — running the trails and filming them for my Join me… series. Nobody asked me to make those. I make them because the light on that coast at 7am deserves better than a phone in someone’s pocket.

That’s the whole reason this page exists. I got good at catching the moment where someone’s legs are burning and they’re grinning anyway, and it turns out that’s the same moment that matters at a race finish line, at a kid’s birthday, and across a café table on a Sunday morning.

I’m also a dad of two, so I know exactly how a kids’ party actually goes — loud, fast, and never once in the order anybody planned. And I’ve spent more than ten years running creative and e-commerce brands, so when a café owner says “I need something for Instagram this week,” I know what that really means: shots you can post on a Tuesday without redesigning your whole feed.

Photo and video sit alongside the websites I build at White Cloud. Same person, same standard, same phone number.
