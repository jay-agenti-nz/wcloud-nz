how it works

Show your code. Snap what you got. That's it.

No paper cards to lose, and no special equipment for the shop. Staff scan your code in the Peelie app on the shop's own phone or iPad.

one

Show your code

At the counter, open Peelie and tap Get my sticker. Your QR code comes up.

two

Staff scan it

They scan your code (or type the one under it) and the sticker's on your card. No location needed.

three

Snap what you got

Take a photo of your coffee, your lunch or your new find, and it becomes your sticker. Or pick one of ours.

fill the card

Every sticker gets you closer to a treat.

Each shop sets its own reward: a free coffee, a pastry, $10 off a toy. When your card's full, tap Claim my reward and show your code again. The treat is yours.

  • Some shops add little rewards along the way
  • Peelie tells you when a reward is ready
  • Your full cards stay in your wallet, so nothing's lost

for cafés, salons, studios & shops

Replace your paper card. Set up in minutes.

A loyalty program your regulars will actually use, with no scanners, no till changes and nothing new to buy. Just install the Peelie app on the phone or iPad you already have behind the counter, and your regulars already carry their card in their pocket.

Design your card

Pick a look, your colour or a photo, the reward, how many stickers fill a card, and a minimum spend.

Scan to give a sticker

Staff check in once a shift with their own PIN, then scan the customer's code. You can see who gave what.

Analytics in plain English

Visits, your busiest day, and where customers drop off, written as a short story about your month.

Ads & offers

Post offers that show on your card. Send one as a notification to customers who've switched on your promotions, up to twice a week.

A poster for your window

Print a poster with your own QR code. Customers scan it and your card's on their phone.

Cheat-proof

Only your staff can give stickers, one per customer every 3 hours, with a log of every shift and an alert if anything looks unusual.

made to feel like yours

A card that looks like your shop.

Choose from designs like Simple, Bistro, Garden, Waves, Gallery and Sunrise, then add your colours, logo or a photo. Swipe the card to see the back, with your address and links.

When you've got something on, write an ad in a minute and it shows on your card. New shops are checked by the Peelie team before they can post.

privacy, done properly

Shops see your code, not you.

When staff scan you, they see a customer code and your stickers with them. They only see your name and contact details if you switch on sharing for that shop. Promotions are off until you turn them on, and your photos are just for you.

Your location is only used on your phone and never sent to us. No ads, no tracking, and you can delete your account in the app at any time. Read the privacy policy.
