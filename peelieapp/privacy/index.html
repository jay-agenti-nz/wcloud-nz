Peelie privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This explains what the Peelie app collects, why, where it's kept, who can see it, and how to delete it. We've written it to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version

  • We collect what Peelie needs to run your stamp cards, and nothing for advertising.
  • When staff scan you, the shop sees a customer code and your stickers with them, not your name. They only see your details if you switch on sharing for that shop.
  • Your sticker photos are private to you.
  • Your location is only used on your phone. We never receive or store it.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, no advertising networks, no analytics companies, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account, and everything linked to it, from inside the app.

Who we are

Peelie is made by White Cloud, a trading name of GROOV Creator Limited, a New Zealand company based in Christchurch (NZBN 9429049024326). We're responsible for the personal information Peelie collects.

For anything in this policy, email teampeelie@wcloud.nz, or in the app go to Settings → Contact Peelie.

Who can use Peelie

You need to be 13 or older to create a Peelie account. If you're under 16, check with a parent or guardian first.

What we collect from customers

Location. Location is optional. If you turn it on, Peelie uses it only on your phone while the app is open, to show “You're at [shop]” so the right card pops up at the counter, and to show shops near you on the Explore map. It is never sent to our servers or stored. Stickers work with location off.

What we collect from shop owners

  • Your account email, and your shop's details: name, area, address, phone, website, social links, the shop's location, logo, photos, menu files, card design, and the ads and posts you publish. Shop details, ads and posts are shown publicly in the app.
  • Your staff members' first names. Staff PINs are only ever stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody, including us, can read them.
  • A shift log: which staff member was on the counter, and when.
  • If you buy Peelie Pro, a record from Apple of your subscription status and renewal date. Payment is handled by Apple, and Peelie never sees card or bank details.

What shops can see about customers

  • Your visits, stickers and reward claims at their own shop, shown with your customer code.
  • Your name, email, phone and birthday only if you switch on sharing for that shop in Settings → Who can see my name. It's off by default and you can switch it off any time. Once a shop has your details, it's responsible for looking after them under the Privacy Act.
  • Shops can only send promotion notifications to customers who've switched on promotion alerts for that shop. These are off by default, and a shop can send at most two a week.

Shops never see your sticker photos, your location, or your stickers at other shops.

What we don't do

  • We don't store your location.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't use advertising networks or analytics companies in the app.
  • We don't sell your information.

Services we use

A few trusted companies process information for us, only to run Peelie:

  • Supabase stores Peelie's database, sign-ins and files in its Sydney, Australia data centre (supabase.com/privacy).
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, push notifications and Peelie Pro payments (apple.com/legal/privacy).
  • Resend sends our sign-in emails (resend.com/legal/privacy-policy).

This means some of your information leaves New Zealand. We only use providers that are bound to protect it to standards comparable to the New Zealand Privacy Act.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have a Peelie account, so your diary stays complete for as long as you want it. You can download your diary (photos and stickers) at any time from Settings.

Deleting your account

Customers: in the app, go to Settings → Delete my account. Your cards, stickers, photos and diary are removed for good.

Shop owners: go to your shop's Settings → Delete my account. This closes your shop on Peelie (its card, customers' stickers there, ads and staff) and deletes your account. If you have Peelie Pro, also cancel it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions so it doesn't renew.

When you delete your account, we delete your data from our systems straight away. Copies may remain in our hosting provider's encrypted backups for up to 30 days, after which they are overwritten.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it. Email teampeelie@wcloud.nz and we'll reply within 20 working days. There's no charge.

If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

This website

This policy covers the Peelie app. These web pages are part of wcloud.nz, which is covered by White Cloud's website privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects information you've already given us, we'll tell you in the app first.
