Collecting stickers

How do I get a sticker?

When you pay, open Peelie at the café, tap Get my sticker and hand your phone to the staff. They type their 4-digit PIN and the sticker's on your card.

When do I take the photo?

Whenever your food or drink arrives. Peelie reminds you a few minutes after you get your sticker. Snap your dish and Peelie cuts it out into a sticker.

If you forget, no problem: after 24 hours the empty spot becomes the café's own sticker, so you never lose a stamp.

How do I claim my reward?

When a card is full, tap Claim my reward at the café and hand your phone to the staff. They type their PIN, just like a sticker, and your treat is yours.

Why can't I get another sticker yet?

It's one sticker per café every 3 hours. That keeps things fair for the café, and means a sticker is for a visit, not each item.

Why does Peelie want my location?

Only while the app is open, to show cafés near you and to check you're at the café when you get a sticker. Peelie never tracks you in the background. If location is off, you won't be able to collect stickers until you turn it back on in your iPhone's Settings → Peelie → Location.

The PIN pad says it's locked

After 5 wrong PINs, the pad locks for 15 minutes on that phone. It's there so nobody can guess a staff member's PIN. Just try again a bit later.

Do I lose my stickers if I change phones?

No. Everything is saved to your account. Sign in on your new phone the same way you did before and it's all there.

Is Peelie on Android?

Not yet. Peelie is coming to iPhone first.

Your privacy

Can the café see who I am?

Only if you switch on Share my name & email for that café. Otherwise they just see counts. You can switch it off any time. Cafés never see your food photos.

How do I stop deals and notifications?

Deal alerts are off unless you turn them on, café by café. You can switch them off on the café's card in the app, or turn off all Peelie notifications in your iPhone's Settings.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings and tap Delete my account. Your account, photos, cards and stickers are permanently removed. See our privacy policy for the details.

For café & restaurant owners

How do I get my café on Peelie?

We're starting with a small group of Christchurch cafés and restaurants. Email kiaora@wcloud.nz with your café's name and we'll be in touch to help you set up.

Do I need any equipment?

No. Staff type their PIN on the customer's own phone. No scanners, tablets or till changes. You can print a window poster with your café's QR code straight from the app.

A staff member forgot their PIN, or has left

In the app, go to Staff and reset or remove their PIN. For security, a PIN is only shown once, when it's created or reset.

How does Peelie stop cheating?

PINs are scrambled and lock after 5 wrong tries, the keypad is shuffled so nobody can learn a PIN by watching, there's one sticker per customer every 3 hours, the customer's phone has to be at your café, and you'll get an alert if one PIN gives out an unusual number of stickers.

How many notifications can I send?

Up to two a week, and only to customers who've switched on deals from you. Keeping it light means people don't turn them off.
