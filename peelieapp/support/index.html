Collecting stickers

How do I get a sticker?

At the counter, tap the Sticker button (or Get my sticker on a shop's card). Your QR code comes up. Staff scan it with the shop's phone or iPad, and the sticker lands on your card. If their camera won't scan, they can type the code under the QR instead.

When do I take the photo?

Whenever your food arrives. Take a photo and Peelie turns it into a sticker, or pick one of ours instead. If you don't get to it, after 24 hours the spot becomes the shop's own sticker, so you never lose a stamp.

How do I claim my reward?

When a reward is ready, open the shop's card and tap Claim my reward. Show your code to the staff and they'll scan it, just like a sticker.

Why can't I get another sticker yet?

It's one sticker per shop every 3 hours. That keeps things fair for the shop, and means a sticker is for a visit, not for each item.

Why does Peelie ask for my location?

It's optional. Peelie uses it on your phone while the app is open, to show shops near you and, if you turn on Card pops up at the shop, to bring up the right card when you walk in. Your location isn't sent to us or to shops, and you can still collect stickers with it off.

Do I lose my stickers if I change phones?

No. Everything is saved to your account. Sign in on your new phone the same way you did before and it's all there. You can also download your diary from Settings.

Is Peelie on Android?

Not yet. Peelie is coming to iPhone and iPad first.

Your privacy

Can the shop see who I am?

Not unless you want them to. When they scan you they see a customer code and your stickers with them. They only see your name and email if you turn on sharing for that shop, in Settings → Who can see my name. Shops never see your food photos.

How do I stop promotions and notifications?

Promotions from a shop are off unless you turn them on. You can change them shop by shop in Settings → Promotions, and switch photo reminders and reward alerts on or off there too.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings and tap Delete my account. Your account, photos, cards and stickers are permanently removed. See our privacy policy for the details.

For shop owners

How do I get my shop on Peelie?

We're starting with a small group of Christchurch cafés, bakeries and eateries. Email kiaora@wcloud.nz with your shop's name and we'll be in touch to help you set up.

Do I need any equipment?

Just a phone or iPad with a camera at the counter, which most shops already have. No scanners or till changes. You can print a window poster with your shop's QR code straight from the app.

How do staff give stickers?

At the start of a shift, each staff member types their own PIN on the shop's phone or iPad. After that they just scan customers' codes and tap Give sticker. Every sticker is logged against whoever was on shift, and they can check out when they finish.

A staff member forgot their PIN, or has left

In the app, go to Staff and reset or remove their PIN. For security, a PIN is only shown once, when it's created or reset. After 5 wrong tries the PIN pad locks for 15 minutes on that device.

How does Peelie stop cheating?

Only signed-in staff on your own device can give stickers. There's one sticker per customer every 3 hours, a log of every shift, a shuffled PIN pad so nobody can learn a PIN by watching, and an alert if one staff member gives out an unusual number of stickers.

How do ads and notifications work?

Ads are free and unlimited, and show on your card in the app. You can also send one as a notification to customers who've turned on your promotions, up to twice a week, so it never feels spammy.
